The Metro Chris standard

Nice buildings don't repair cars.People do.

A professional workshop is not the reception desk. It is what happens to your vehicle after the keys are handed over.

01

Diagnose first.

We listen to the symptom and investigate the cause before replacing parts.

02

Explain the repair.

You should understand what is being done and why. No mystery work.

03

Ask before the job changes.

If the repair needs to change, the conversation happens before the extra work.

04

Repair. Check. Test.

The aim is not to finish quickly. The aim is to return the vehicle properly sorted.
